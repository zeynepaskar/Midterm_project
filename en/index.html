More than a museum

The Military-Historical Museum is part of the National Military-Patriotic Center of the Armed Forces. Its building does more than hold exhibits: it also contains a concert hall, a hall for temporary exhibitions, and it is home to the Central Military Orchestra and the Central Ensemble of the Ministry of Defence.

The museum describes its work as preserving military heritage, research, museum education and patriotic education. Its exhibitions present military history from ancient times to the modern period through archaeological objects, weapons, documents, uniforms, works of art and military equipment.

Quick facts

  • Location: Astana, Republic Square area
  • Opened as a museum: 2015, according to Visit Astana; the building complex dates from 1998
  • Structure: seven main exhibition halls plus an outdoor military equipment display
  • Collection: thousands of objects — published estimates differ by source

Why this museum matters

The museum combines preservation, research and education. A 2025 academic study describes the Astana Military-Historical Museum as a specialised museum that presents the memory of the Great Patriotic War and the wider military history of Kazakhstan.

Its collection can be explored not only as a group of objects, but also as a way to study changes in military organisation, weapons, military art and historical memory.
