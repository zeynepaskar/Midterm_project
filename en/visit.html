Tips for your visit

  1. Start at the atrium and follow the halls in order — the exhibition is chronological.
  2. Look for the temporary-exhibitions hall: its programme changes.
  3. Leave the outdoor pavilion for the end and, if you can, for good weather.
  4. Phone ahead to confirm opening hours and the current ticket price.
