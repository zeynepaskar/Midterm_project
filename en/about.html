About the Museum

A state museum devoted to the military heritage of Kazakhstan — and much more than a row of showcases.

What is the museum?

The Military-Historical Museum of the Armed Forces of the Republic of Kazakhstan is a specialised museum devoted to military heritage. Visit Astana describes it as a state museum opened in 2015 and notes that its exhibitions follow the chronology of Kazakhstan’s military history.

The e-Museum description presents the institution as a scientific, educational and cultural centre whose work includes preservation of military heritage, research, museum education and patriotic education.

Architecture and space

The museum occupies a large blue-and-white building that is part of a wider architectural complex. E-Museum dates the complex to 1998 and notes that, besides the exhibition halls, it contains a concert hall, a temporary-exhibitions hall and the Central Orchestra and Central Ensemble of the Ministry of Defence. A scale model in the museum shows the whole layout, dome included.

The two dates are not a contradiction: the building was completed in 1998, while Visit Astana gives 2015 as the year the museum opened in its present form.

The collection

The collection includes archaeological finds, weapons, military uniforms, documents, works of art and rare historical artifacts. Published figures differ, so both are shown here rather than choosing one:

  • 10,000+ — objects, according to e-Museum
  • ≈20,000 — objects, according to Visit Astana

Whichever number is closer, the range is wide — from a single helmet on a pedestal to a parade car and missile launchers in the open air.

The museum as an educational space

Military history is broader than a list of battles. Academic research identifies five connected areas, and the halls give visitors a way into each of them.

History of wars
Campaigns, battles and their consequences.
Military art
Strategy and tactics — how wars are planned and fought.
Armed forces
How military organisations are formed, staffed and developed.
Military technology
Weapons and equipment, from bows to armoured vehicles.
Military thought
The ideas and theories behind military decisions.

This approach is reflected in the museum’s halls: they let visitors study weapons, military organisation, historical events, visual art and the development of the Armed Forces of Kazakhstan.
