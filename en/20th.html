Military History of the XX Century

Stop 3 of 10

The museum at its most human: maps, helmets, posters and personal belongings from the wars that shaped the twentieth century.

From the early twentieth century to Afghanistan

The museum’s twentieth-century hall covers major military and political periods including the Alash period, the Great Patriotic War and the participation of citizens of Kazakhstan in the war in Afghanistan. E-Museum specifically identifies these themes as part of the hall.

Stalingrad and Leningrad

Two of the largest display cases are given to the defence of Stalingrad and Leningrad. Panels of maps and photographs stand behind helmets, mess tins, field equipment and everyday belongings. The Leningrad case even includes a concert poster for Shostakovich’s Seventh Symphony — a reminder that culture was part of the besieged city’s resistance.

Өмір мен өлім арасындағы заттар

Heading of a museum display photographed in the hall — in English, “Objects between life and death”.

Kazakhstan and the Great Patriotic War

Research published in 2024 examines military formations created in Soviet Kazakhstan during 1941–1942. The study reports that more than 50 units and formations were formed in Kazakhstan during this period, including rifle divisions, national cavalry divisions and brigades.

The same study describes the formation of the 310th, 312th, 314th and 316th rifle divisions in Akmolinsk, Aktobe, Petropavlovsk and Alma-Ata.

Historical research

Academic research on military history in Kazakhstan treats the history of wars, military art, armed forces, military technology and military thought as interconnected fields.
